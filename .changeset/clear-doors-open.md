The connect screen has a new look and buttons. It uses the Handset icon and colours and a proper typeface instead of pixel letters. **Wi-Fi** lists paired phones found on your network, lets you type an address, and switches a USB phone to Wi-Fi. **QR code** shows a code to scan on the phone (Wireless debugging, Pair device with QR code) and pairs and connects without typing.
